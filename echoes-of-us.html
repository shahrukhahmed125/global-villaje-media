Community & Legacy Programme

Echoes of Us

Preserving memory, identity and lived experience through storytelling.

Echoes of Us helps individuals, families and communities record and preserve stories of identity, migration, family, care, resilience and belonging through guided conversations, story circles, audio and film.

Explore How It Works Commission or Support Echoes of Us
Preserving Living Heritage

Stories that connect generations and communities

Every family and community carries memories, knowledge and experiences that deserve to be preserved. Echoes of Us creates supportive opportunities for people to reflect, share and record the stories that shaped who they are.

More than recording a memory

  • Preserve personal and community history.
  • Strengthen intergenerational connection.
  • Recognise lived experience.
  • Build community connection.
  • Create a lasting audio, film or digital record.
Programme Pathways

Two ways to experience Echoes of Us

Community Storytelling Programme

A structured programme delivered with a community organisation, care provider, local authority, charity or cultural group, including story circles, individual recordings, edited stories and a sharing event.

Illustrative community programme:

  • Story Circle One: Where We Come From.
  • Story Circle Two: Care, Family and Responsibility.
  • Story Circle Three: Belonging.
  • Six professionally edited audio stories.
  • Eight short social-media clips.
  • One community listening or sharing event.
  • A digital story page or collection.
  • A short activity and learning report.

The final structure and outputs are agreed with the commissioner and adapted to participants, consent, budget and purpose.

Individual and Family Legacy Stories

A personal service for individuals or families wishing to record a life story, preserve memories or create a lasting audio or video legacy.

Individual and family outputs:

  • A recorded life-story conversation.
  • A professionally edited audio story.
  • A short legacy film.
  • An intergenerational family conversation.
  • A private digital recording for family members.

Participants & Commissioners

Who can take part or commission?

Echoes of Us can support older adults, families, intergenerational groups, Black African and Caribbean communities, diaspora communities, unpaid carers, cultural groups and anyone wishing to preserve a significant life story.

Commissioners may include councils, NHS and public-health organisations, charities, care homes, carers’ organisations, housing associations, cultural organisations, universities, community groups and faith organisations.

Structured Methodology

How it works

A step-by-step collaborative process ensuring contributor comfort, editorial care, and cultural respect.

01

Initial Conversation

Initial conversation about purpose, participants, audience and format.

02

Planning & Consent

Planning of themes, questions, consent and recording parameters.

03

Facilitated Sessions

Individual, family or story-circle sessions guided with empathy.

04

Professional Production

Professional audio/video recording, sound design, and master editing.

05

Participant Review

Participant review where appropriate before final sign-off.

06

Delivery & Sharing

Private delivery or agreed community listening and digital sharing.

Stories shared with permission and respect

Participants decide what they want to share and can identify subjects they do not wish to discuss. GVM explains the intended use, audience and privacy arrangements before recording, and takes particular care with bereavement, trauma, ill health and caring experiences.

Echoes of Us is a storytelling and creative-media programme. It is not a clinical therapy or counselling service.
Commission Programme

Whose story should be preserved?

A story does not have to be famous to be valuable. The memories, knowledge and experiences carried within families and communities deserve the opportunity to be heard and remembered.
