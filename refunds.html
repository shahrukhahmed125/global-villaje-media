Commissioning Policy

Commission, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

Policy governing commissioning contracts, broadcast sponsorships, pilot programmes, milestone payments, cancellations and rescheduling.

Notice: This policy is currently under review and has not been formally approved. Please contact GVM at info@globalvillajemedia.org for queries.

1. Principle of Bespoke Production

Global Villaje Media delivers custom social-impact media, broadcast productions, community workshops, and health campaigns. Because our services involve dedicated studio booking, research preparation, professional contributor coordination, and production scheduling, commissioning arrangements are managed under transparent milestone structures.

2. Milestone Payments & Invoicing

Commissioned services (including The Health Hour sponsorships, Caring with Confidence™ pilots, Campaign in a Box, and Echoes of Us community programmes) are typically invoiced on a structured milestone basis (e.g. initial inception deposit, delivery milestone, and final report delivery) as stipulated in each commissioning contract.

3. Rescheduling Policy

We understand that unforeseen community, public health, or contributor circumstances may arise.

  • Studio & Broadcast Recordings: Notice of rescheduling must be provided in writing at least 7 business days in advance to allow for radio slot re-allocation at no penalty.
  • Community Story Circles & In-Person Workshops: Notice of rescheduling should be provided at least 10 business days in advance. We will work flexibly with the commissioning partner to agree upon an alternate delivery date.

4. Cancellation and Contract Termination

If a commissioning organisation or sponsor terminates an agreed project:

  • Prior to Work Inception: If cancelled prior to the commencement of research, script development, or studio preparation, any deposit paid is refunded minus an administrative fee of 15%.
  • During Active Production: Once production, contributor coordination, or studio recording has commenced, payment is due for all completed milestone stages and incurred direct third-party production costs.
  • Completed Deliverables: Deliverables that have been completed, recorded, edited, or broadcast in accordance with the agreed Statement of Work are non-refundable.

5. Quality Assurance and Corrections

Global Villaje Media provides designated review periods for all audio/video edits, campaign artwork, and written toolkits prior to final sign-off. Necessary factual corrections, editorial refinements, or clinical adjustments identified during the review window will be executed promptly at no extra charge.

6. Contact Regarding Commissioning Terms

For questions regarding commissioning contracts or cancellation notices, please contact our management team:
