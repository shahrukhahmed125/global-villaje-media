Health-Literacy & Carers Empowerment

Caring with Confidence™

Supporting unpaid carers, families and the people they care for through accessible, culturally responsive health information.

Caring with Confidence™ is a health-literacy, prevention and empowerment programme designed to help people feel better informed, more confident and better able to navigate caring responsibilities, health appointments and support services.

Available for discovery, co-design and pilot commissioning.
Explore the Foundation Pilot Discuss a Local Pilot
The Case for Change

Why Caring with Confidence™ is needed

Many people do not initially identify themselves as carers. They see themselves as a husband, wife, daughter, son, parent, sibling or friend who is simply helping someone they love.

As caring responsibilities grow, people may need to understand health conditions, medication, appointments, services, benefits and emergency planning while also protecting their own health. Information is often scattered, difficult to interpret or not communicated in a culturally relevant way.

Caring with Confidence™ brings practical learning, trusted conversation and signposting together in one adaptable programme. It complements existing carers’ services and helps organisations address health-literacy and service-navigation gaps.

Beneficiaries & Carers

Who the programme supports

People providing unpaid support because of illness, disability, mental ill health or age.
Family members who do not yet recognise themselves as carers.
People living with long-term conditions and the relatives or friends supporting them.
Black African and Caribbean carers and families experiencing cultural or communication barriers.
Non-Black carers supporting a Black family member or friend.
Wider communities where health inequalities, access barriers or low health literacy are present.
Turnkey Pilot

The Foundation Pilot

The Foundation Pilot provides a practical starting point that can be adapted to local needs, existing services and commissioner priorities.

Six to eight short audio-learning modules.
One live online learning session.
One in-person learning session.
One professionally hosted health conversation.
Practical summaries, checklists and signposting.
Carer, family and stakeholder feedback.
Pre- and post-programme evaluation.
A concise learning and impact report.

The final number, length and format of activities will be agreed with the commissioning organisation.

A Manageable First Step

Discovery and Co-design Session

Organisations that are not ready to commission a full pilot can begin with a professionally facilitated Discovery and Co-design Session.

  • One facilitated listening and insight session.
  • Participation from carers, family members, staff or relevant community stakeholders.
  • Identification of health-literacy and service-navigation gaps.
  • Consideration of cultural and communication needs.
  • Five practical recommendations.
  • A short insight summary.
  • An outline for a locally adapted Foundation Pilot.

The session can draw on a manageable group and existing organisational knowledge. It does not require a large-scale carer recruitment exercise before work can begin.

Cultural Grounding

Culturally responsive by design

Cultural responsiveness is embedded throughout Caring with Confidence™. The programme considers how culture, identity, family expectations, faith, language, stigma, trust and previous experiences of services can affect how people understand information and seek support.

GVM has particular expertise in engaging Black African and Caribbean communities, while the model can be adapted for diverse communities and local circumstances.

Strategic Impact

Value for commissioners and partners

  • Supports prevention and earlier action.
  • Improves confidence in using health information.
  • Strengthens preparation for appointments and conversations.
  • Complements existing carers’ provision.
  • Creates reusable, accessible learning content.
  • Provides structured community insight and feedback.
  • Supports culturally responsive engagement.
  • Generates practical learning for future service development.
Robust Methodology

Delivery and evaluation

GVM will agree the audience, location, learning priorities, recruitment approach, delivery partners and intended outcomes with the commissioner. Evaluation can include participation, completion, confidence, knowledge, usefulness and intended behaviour change, supported by qualitative feedback.

Important Disclaimer: Caring with Confidence™ provides education and signposting. It does not replace individual medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, legal advice or statutory carer assessment.
