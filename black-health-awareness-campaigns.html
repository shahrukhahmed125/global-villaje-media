Inequalities & Representation

Black Health Awareness Campaigns

Culturally responsive health communication addressing health inequalities.

Global Villaje Media creates targeted campaigns, broadcast conversations and community resources to raise awareness of health conditions that disproportionately affect Black African and Caribbean communities.

Priority Health Themes Commission a Campaign
Health Equity

Why culturally responsive campaigns matter

Health disparities are well documented. In the UK, Black African and Caribbean people experience higher rates of certain conditions, including prostate cancer, hypertension, stroke, type 2 diabetes and sickle cell, and face distinct barriers in mental health, dementia care and maternal health.

Standard public-health campaigns do not always reflect the language, cultural context, trusted channels or lived reality of the communities they need to reach. GVM combines professional health communication with community insight to produce campaigns that are clear, respectful and effective.

Focus Areas

Priority health themes

Prostate cancer and men's health

1 in 4 Black men will get prostate cancer in their lifetime. Earlier awareness and risk discussions save lives.

High blood pressure & cardiovascular

Hypertension, stroke risk, regular testing and lifestyle changes made culturally relevant.

Type 2 diabetes and prevention

Culturally adapted nutritional advice, active living and earlier screening pathways.

Sickle cell and blood donation

Raising awareness of sickle cell management and the urgent need for Ro-subtype blood donors.

Maternal health & perinatal care

Addressing maternal mortality disparities and empowering expectant mothers and birthing people.

Dementia & unpaid caring

Reducing stigma, supporting carers and promoting earlier diagnosis in African & Caribbean families.

Mental health & emotional wellbeing

Normalising conversations about stress, depression and seeking help without fear of prejudice.

Health research & representation

Building trust and understanding in clinical trials, genomic medicine and public health studies.

Our Methodology

How GVM approaches Black health awareness

  • Grounded in trusted clinical information.
  • Informed by lived experience and community insight.
  • Delivered through accessible audio, video, broadcast and digital formats.
  • Partnered with community organisations, places of worship and local advocates.
  • Focused on practical action: getting checked, asking questions and accessing services.
Ethical Standards

Responsible communication principles

GVM uses verified public-health guidance, clinical evidence and NHS or charity information where applicable.

GVM does not promote unverified medical treatments, promote fear or claim that all members of a community share identical health beliefs or experiences.

Every campaign is co-designed to bridge the gap between healthcare institutions and the communities they serve with absolute professional integrity.
