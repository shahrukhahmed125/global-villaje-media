Broadcast Programme

The Health, Wellness & Wellbeing Hour

Trusted health conversations that inform, connect and give communities a voice.

Produced and hosted by Global Villaje Media and broadcast through Chalkhill Community Radio, the programme brings together health professionals, community organisations, advocates and people with lived experience for accessible conversations about health.

Accessible Communication

Health information through human conversation

Health information is often communicated through reports, websites and leaflets. These resources are important, but they do not always answer people’s questions, reflect their experiences or build the trust needed for meaningful engagement.

Each programme combines professional insight, lived experience and practical information. Complex issues are explored in clear language, with particular attention to health inequalities and conditions that disproportionately affect Black African and Caribbean communities.

More than an interview

  • Improve health literacy.
  • Amplify lived experience.
  • Build understanding and trust.
  • Address barriers to prevention, diagnosis, treatment and support.
  • Extend selected conversations through audio, video and social-media content.

Respectful, responsible and culturally informed

GVM prepares contributors, explains the format, agrees appropriate boundaries and aims to create a respectful space where people can speak without being sensationalised. Lived experience is presented as the contributor’s account; GVM does not make clinical, legal or investigative findings.

Programme content is intended for education and discussion and does not replace individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
