Narrative Depth

The power of long-form human documentary

Documentaries allow complex health, social and cultural issues to be explored with the depth, context and human nuance they deserve. They give voice to lived experience, create an enduring historical record and generate empathy and insight that short-form media cannot always achieve.

Approaches to documentary production:

  • Commissioned Documentaries: Developed for or in partnership with organisations seeking to explore a specific theme, community or milestone.
  • Development and Independent Projects: GVM-originated projects exploring subjects of cultural, social and health significance.
