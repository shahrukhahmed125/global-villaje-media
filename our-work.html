Our Work

What we do, how we deliver it and the organisations we collaborate with.

Global Villaje Media develops culturally responsive health communication, community storytelling, media campaigns and educational programmes that bridge the divide between health systems and underserved communities.

Operational Scope

Our four areas of activity

1. Broadcast Health Conversations

Accessible, human health discussions bringing health professionals, community organisations and people with lived experience together via The Health Hour and digital channels.

2. Community Storytelling

Preserving living memory, intergenerational connection, diaspora journeys and personal resilience through Echoes of Us story circles and legacy audio.

3. Carers' Health Literacy

Empowering unpaid carers and families through Caring with Confidence™ audio learning modules, live co-design sessions and practical service navigation.

4. Multimedia Campaigns

Unified, culturally responsive health communication packages through Campaign in a Box and Black Health Awareness initiatives.

Published Broadcast Conversation

More Than a Workshop: Building Health, Trust and Sisterhood

Context & Delivery: A dedicated 50-minute broadcast on Chalkhill Community Radio bringing grassroots facilitators and health advocates together to discuss maternal health inequalities, emotional wellbeing and cultural barriers.

Radio broadcast + Digital podcast release + 3 social-media video clips.

Targeted Awareness Campaign

Prostate Cancer UK: Raising Risk Awareness in Black Men

Context & Delivery: A targeted campaign addressing the 1 in 4 lifetime prostate cancer risk for Black men. Combined professional health insights with lived-experience testimonials to encourage PSA blood testing and reduce cultural fear.

Flagship broadcast + Community flyer distribution + Health Q&A.

Collaborative Delivery Network

Global Villaje Media collaborates with broadcast partners like Chalkhill Community Radio, NHS public-health leads, local authority community engagement teams, voluntary sector carers organisations, and African and Caribbean diaspora groups.

Editorial Transparency: We clearly distinguish between completed published broadcasts, structured foundation pilot offers (such as Caring with Confidence™), and projects in creative development (such as our documentary treatments).
