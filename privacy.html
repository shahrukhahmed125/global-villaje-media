Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

How Global Villaje Media collects, protects and handles personal data and contributor recordings in compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Notice: This policy is currently under review and has not been formally approved. Please contact GVM at info@globalvillajemedia.org for queries.

1. Introduction and Overview

Global Villaje Media ("GVM", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data rights of our website visitors, clients, commissioning partners, and broadcast contributors. This Privacy Policy details how we handle personal data when you interact with our website (www.globalvillajemedia.org), participate in our broadcast recordings, commission our programmes, or communicate with us.

2. Data Controller Contact Information

The Data Controller responsible for your personal information is:

  • Entity: Global Villaje Media
  • Lead Officer: Charles Ogunnowo Olorunfemi, Founder & General Manager
  • Email: info@globalvillajemedia.org
  • Telephone: 07915 697589
  • Jurisdiction: United Kingdom

3. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Enquiry & Commissioning Information: Name, professional title, organisation name, business email, telephone number, and message details submitted via our contact forms.
  • Contributor Audio & Video Data: Voice recordings, video footage, photographs, and personal statements recorded during interviews, story circles, or broadcast sessions, collected solely on the basis of written consent.
  • Technical Website Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and interaction data collected anonymously via essential cookies for performance and security.

4. Lawful Bases for Processing

We process personal information under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: Where you have provided unambiguous consent to participate in a recording, receive communications, or share personal stories.
  • Contractual Necessity: Where processing is necessary to prepare, execute, or deliver a commissioning agreement or pilot programme.
  • Legitimate Interests: To manage, operate, protect, and improve our services, communications, and web security.

5. Audio, Video and Contributor Consent

Contributor privacy is paramount. Before any recording begins for The Health, Wellness & Wellbeing Hour, Echoes of Us, or our documentary projects, contributors are fully briefed on the intended audience, scope, and distribution channels. Written consent agreements are signed prior to publication, and contributors retain the right to clarify or withdraw personal remarks prior to master editing sign-off.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share necessary data with trusted service providers who adhere to strict UK GDPR standards (e.g. secure cloud hosting, media delivery networks, and our broadcast partner Chalkhill Community Radio solely for agreed programming).

7. Data Retention & Security

We retain client and contact enquiry records only for as long as necessary to manage our business relationship or fulfil legal requirements. Contributor master archives are maintained securely and reviewed periodically. We implement robust physical, technical, and organisational security measures to protect all data against unauthorised access or loss.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal or archival limitations).
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

9. Policy Updates

This policy was last updated in 2026. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
