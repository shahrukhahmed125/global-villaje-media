Turnkey Health Campaigns

Campaign in a Box

A focused multimedia campaign designed for practical community delivery.

Campaign in a Box helps organisations turn an important health message into a coordinated set of accessible video, audio, digital and community-facing materials.

Explore the Package Discuss a Campaign
Unified Messaging

One message. Multiple formats. Coordinated delivery.

Important health information is often distributed as isolated leaflets, posts or videos. Messages can become inconsistent, difficult to share or disconnected from the communities they are intended to reach.

Campaign in a Box brings the essential campaign elements together around one health issue, audience and objective. GVM develops the core story, coordinates the content and prepares materials that partners can use across community and digital channels.

Package Elements

What a Campaign in a Box can include

Final outputs are tailored to the campaign objective, audience, budget, clinical-information requirements and distribution plan.

One flagship health conversation or video.
Up to six short-form video clips.
A coordinated set of branded information graphics.
Short audio explainers.
Promotional captions and suggested social posts.
Printable or QR-linked community materials.
A simple partner distribution guide.
Signposting to trusted services.
A basic activity, engagement and learning report.

Illustrative Example

Illustrative campaign: dementia awareness

A locally adapted dementia Campaign in a Box could help Black African and Caribbean families recognise possible signs, understand where to seek advice and begin conversations earlier.

  • One flagship video combining trusted information and community perspective.
  • Six short clips addressing common questions or misconceptions.
  • Ten coordinated awareness graphics.
  • QR cards for barbers, hair salons, faith settings and community venues.
  • Radio or audio content.
  • Local service signposting.
  • Partner sharing materials.
Note: This is an illustrative model and should not be presented as a completed or commissioned campaign unless that status changes.
Local Customization

Designed for local adaptation

Campaign in a Box is not a one-size-fits-all template. Language, contributors, imagery, distribution routes and signposting can be adapted to a particular place, population or service pathway.

Measurement and learning

Depending on scope, GVM can record outputs delivered, partner participation, distribution activity, digital engagement and qualitative feedback. The report will distinguish between reach and engagement and will not claim health outcomes that the campaign has not measured.

Who Can Commission

Who can commission it?

  • NHS organisations and public-health teams.
  • Local authorities.
  • Health and carers’ charities.
  • Research organisations.
  • Care providers.
  • Housing associations.
  • Community and faith organisations.
  • Responsible businesses supporting health or community priorities.
