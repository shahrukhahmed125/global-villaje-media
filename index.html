Founder's Vision & Mission

"When Health Information Speaks Your Cultural Language, It Saves Lives."

Charles Ogunnowo Olorunfemi founded Global Villaje Media to resolve a persistent, tragic reality: clinical advice in the UK was often delivered in clinical jargon that alienated Black African and Caribbean communities, fueling distrust and delayed diagnoses.

By combining community radio broadcasting, grassroots film production, culinary sessions, and honest peer conversations, Global Villaje Media creates safe spaces where people listen, learn, and take control of their health.

"Our mission is simple: to make health literacy culturally accessible, dignifying, and engaging for every family in the global village."
Charles Ogunnowo Olorunfemi • Founder & Lead Health Communication Strategist

Everything You Need to Know About GVM

Find clear answers regarding our media productions, NHS partnerships, and community engagement models.

We do not simply translate brochures into different languages. We work from within Black African and Caribbean cultural frameworks—using authentic oral traditions, food, trusted music, faith perspectives, and lived experiences to make health messages deeply relevant and trusted.
NHS organisations can commission us through direct service agreements, community engagement grants, or campaign production contracts. We deliver full end-to-end deliverables including audio spots, video documentaries, evaluation reports, and community reach metrics.
