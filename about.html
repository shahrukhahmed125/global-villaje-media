Company Story & Purpose

About Global Villaje Media

Health communication rooted in trust, representation and community voice.

Who We Are

Bridging the gap with culturally responsive engagement

Global Villaje Media is a UK-based social-impact health media company using radio, video, audio, digital media and community storytelling to improve health literacy, address health inequalities and promote quality health.

GVM helps NHS organisations, charities and public bodies bridge the gap between services and Black African and Caribbean communities through trusted communication, culturally responsive engagement and community storytelling.

We create accessible health conversations, campaigns and programmes that connect professional knowledge with lived experience. Our work pays particular attention to health issues and barriers that disproportionately affect Black communities, while many of our services can be adapted for wider communities experiencing inequality or exclusion.

Context & Social Impact

Why GVM exists

The unequal impact of COVID-19 highlighted how health outcomes are shaped by access to trusted information, representation, confidence in services and wider social conditions. It also reinforced the importance of communication that communities can understand, question and recognise.

GVM was established to bring health and social-care understanding together with community media and culturally grounded storytelling. Our aim is to help important information travel further, create space for voices that are often overlooked and support more meaningful engagement between organisations and communities.

Our Mission

To improve health literacy, address health inequalities and promote quality health through trusted communication, culturally responsive engagement and community storytelling.

Our Vision

A future where every community has equitable access to trusted health information, feels represented in health conversations and is empowered to make informed decisions about health and wellbeing.

Leadership

Charles Ogunnowo Olorunfemi

Founder and General Manager

Charles Ogunnowo Olorunfemi is the Founder/General Manager of Global Villaje Media and host of The Health, Wellness & Wellbeing Hour.

He brings more than 20 years of experience in health and social-care education, assessment and quality assurance, supported by a Foundation Degree in Community Media and a BSc in Creative Industries.

Charles established GVM to combine his understanding of health and care with broadcasting, creative production and community engagement. His work focuses on making health information more accessible, exploring the experiences behind health inequalities and creating respectful spaces where professional and community voices can meet.

Broadcasting Base & Reach

Where we work

GVM broadcasts from Watford and Brent, with local, regional, national and global reach through radio and digital platforms.
