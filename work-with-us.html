Commissioning & Partnerships

Work With Us

Let’s discuss your health communication, community engagement or storytelling priority.

Whether you want to sponsor a broadcast conversation, commission an Echoes of Us project, pilot Caring with Confidence™, or create a Campaign in a Box, we will work with you to shape a practical proposal.

Direct Collaboration

Four principal ways to partner with GVM

1. Health Conversation Sponsorship

Sponsor or commission a dedicated, professionally hosted episode on The Health Hour with radio broadcast & social clips.

2. Echoes of Us Storytelling

Commission a community story circle programme or a private, multi-generational family legacy recording.

3. Caring with Confidence™ Pilot

Commission a local Foundation Pilot or start with a manageable Discovery & Co-design session for unpaid carers.

4. Campaign in a Box or Documentary

Commission a coordinated multimedia package or co-produce an in-depth long-form documentary.
