Programmes and Services

Culturally responsive health communication, community engagement and storytelling designed to inform, connect and create meaningful impact.

Global Villaje Media develops programmes, conversations and multimedia campaigns that improve health literacy, address health inequalities and help communities engage more confidently with health information and services.

Our work can be commissioned as a single activity, a defined campaign, a local pilot or a longer-term programme.

Principal Programmes

Our principal programmes and services

Each offer can be commissioned, sponsored or adapted with NHS organisations, councils, charities, care providers, research teams and other socially responsible organisations.

Broadcast Conversation

The Health, Wellness & Wellbeing Hour

Accessible conversations bringing health professionals, community organisations and people with lived experience together. Organisations can commission or sponsor a single episode, condition-specific conversation, short series or organisational spotlight.

Explore the Programme
Community & Legacy

Echoes of Us

A community and legacy storytelling programme using story circles, individual recordings, audio, film and shared events to preserve memory, identity and belonging.

Explore Echoes of Us
Foundation Pilot

Caring with Confidence™

A culturally responsive health-literacy and empowerment programme for unpaid carers, families and the people they support. The Foundation Pilot is available for commissioning and local adaptation.

Explore Caring with Confidence™
Multimedia Kit

Campaign in a Box

A coordinated multimedia package bringing video, audio, graphics, community materials and distribution support together around one health issue, audience and action.

Explore Campaign in a Box
Targeted Campaigns

Black Health Awareness Campaigns

Targeted campaigns combining trusted information, community insight, lived-experience voices and culturally responsive media around health inequalities affecting Black communities.
